---
name: ai-disclosure-and-ip
description: Rules for AI disclosure, copyright and trademark safety. Use whenever creating prompts, listings or reviewing designs.
---
- Disclose AI involvement as each marketplace requires (Etsy: select the AI-assisted / "made with" option and state it in the description). Check the current policy quarterly — it changes.
- Banned in prompts, titles, tags, descriptions: living or recently deceased artists' names, brands, trademarks (check new words against a trademark search such as USPTO/EUIPO), celebrities, fictional characters, sports teams, logos.
- Human contribution: record edits made (composition, colour work, typography, compositing) in `prompt.md` — strengthens authorship and quality.
- If a design closely resembles a known artwork or photo → fail QA.
- Quote prints: only public-domain or own text; verify attribution.
