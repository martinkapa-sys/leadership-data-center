---
## Brief template (`designs/<SKU>/brief.md`)
```
# <Title>
Theme / subject:
Why now (evidence + link to research report):
Target buyer & room:
Mood words (3–5):
Palette (hex):
Composition notes (must survive 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, ISO crops — keep subject centred with margin):
Collection / set fit:
Must avoid:
Success metric: e.g. ≥ 2% visit→order within 30 days
```
## Rubric (100)
- Demand evidence 25 · Competition gap 20 · Brand fit 20 (style guide) · Collection/bundle potential 15 · Seasonality timing 10 · Production feasibility 10.
- Below 60 → drop. Brand fit < 12 → drop regardless of total.
