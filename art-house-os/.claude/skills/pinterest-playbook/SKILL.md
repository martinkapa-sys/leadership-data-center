---
name: pinterest-playbook
description: Pinterest strategy for wall-art traffic: pins, boards, keywords, cadence. Use for any Pinterest work.
---
- Boards by room and style ("Boho Living Room Wall Art", "Minimalist Bedroom Prints"); keyword-rich board descriptions.
- 3–5 fresh pins per new SKU across its first month (different mockups/crops), 2:3 vertical (1000×1500).
- Pin title = search phrase; description 2–3 sentences with keywords; link to the listing with UTM.
- Cadence 5–15 pins/day spread out; never duplicate identical pins.
- Promote proven pins (high saves) with small ad budgets — via approval.
