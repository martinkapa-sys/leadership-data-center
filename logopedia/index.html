How it works

Each story is a short play. In every scene a new character appears, and your child plays its part by imitating its sound (“Sssss” like a snake, “Rrroar” like a lion). Mouth-and-tongue warm-ups and breathing games are built into the story. Each sound is practised three times.

Magic ears 👂 listen through the microphone and move the story on when your child makes the sound. They hear that a sound was made, not how well it was said, so stay close, show the sound first, and praise effort. You can always tap the golden button instead.

Tips

• Short and often: one story a day is plenty.
• Sit in front of a mirror for the tongue and lip games.
• Make the sound yourself first, then let your child copy you.
• This app supports speech therapy at home. It does not replace a speech therapist.
