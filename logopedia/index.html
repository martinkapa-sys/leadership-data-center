Story Stage

Pick a story. You are the star.

For grown-ups

How it works

Each story is a short play. In every scene a new character walks on stage, and your child plays its part by copying its sound three times. Tongue, lip and breathing games are part of the plot.
Magic ears listen through the microphone and move the story on when your child makes the sound. They hear that a sound was made, not how well. Stay close, show the sound first, and praise effort. The golden button always works too.

Tips

Short and often: one story a day is plenty.
Sit in front of a mirror for the tongue and lip games.
Make the sound yourself first, then let your child copy you.
This app supports speech therapy at home. It does not replace a speech therapist.
