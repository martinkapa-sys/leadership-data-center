---
name: instagram-tiktok-playbook
description: Instagram and TikTok formats, hooks and posting rules for an art print shop. Use when creating short-form or feed content.
---
- Formats: room reveal (before/after), "3 prints for a <room>", gallery wall build, process timelapse, colour story carousel, customer photos (with permission).
- Hook in first 1–2 s / first line. Captions: story → product → CTA (link in bio / shop tag).
- 5–10 relevant hashtags (#wallart #gallerywall #printableart + niche + room).
- Tag products (Instagram Shopping) when the catalog is connected.
