name: weekly-report
description: Weekly KPI report format and recommendations. Use for the Friday growth review.
---
`data/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`:
1. KPIs vs last week: visits, conversion, orders, revenue, AOV, email subscribers, social reach, ad spend & ROAS.
2. Top 5 and bottom 5 SKUs; patterns by theme, style, palette, ratio, price.
3. Pipeline health: count per status, cycle time idea→live.
4. Experiments: decisions with numbers.
5. Recommendations (3–5), each tagged owner agent; spend items → `data/approvals.md`.
6. Retire candidates: live > 90 days, < 50 visits, 0 orders.
