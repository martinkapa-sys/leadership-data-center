# Rollout plan

| Phase | Weeks | Turn on | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | 1–2 | style guide, pricing, catalog, Replicate, shop connector; designer → print-qa → merchandiser (drafts) | 10 SKUs live, QA pass rate ≥ 80% |
| 2. Reach | 3–4 | Pinterest, Instagram, mockups, content calendar | 3+ pins/day, first organic sales |
| 3. Learn | 5–8 | analytics, weekly report, first ad-creative A/B tests | weekly report trusted, 2 experiments decided |
| 4. Autopilot | 9+ | scheduled cycle, Klaviyo flows, customer-care | you spend ≤ 20 min/day on approvals |

Relax an approval gate only after it has been 100% correct for a month (e.g. let approved reply templates go out automatically first).
Your taste is the moat — keep idea approval human.
