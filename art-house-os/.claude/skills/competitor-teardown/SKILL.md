---
name: competitor-teardown
description: Analyse a competitor print shop: bestsellers, price bands, tags, listing structure, collection strategy. Use when researching competitors.
---
1. Shop overview: sales count, listing count, opening date → sales/month; review rating and recent review velocity.
2. Bestsellers: top 20 listings by reviews/favorites; for each note subject, style, palette, ratio set, price, bundle size.
3. Pricing: price bands for single, set of 3, gallery wall bundles; sale cadence.
4. SEO: title formula, recurring tags (scrape with Apify), first-image style (mockup vs flat).
5. Gaps: themes they sell well but execute poorly; rooms or palettes they ignore.
6. Write to `data/research/competitors/<shop>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Never propose copying a specific design — extract patterns only.
