description: Tone and procedures for customer messages and reviews. Use when drafting replies.
---
- Warm, brief, signed with the house name. Answer the question in the first sentence.
- Download issues: resend link steps; offer email delivery. Printing help: link the How-to-print guide + correct ratio file for their frame.
- Custom requests/licensing: capture details, escalate.
- Reviews: thank every 4–5★; for ≤ 3★ draft a solution-first reply for approval.
