---
name: collection-builder
description: Group designs into coherent sets, gallery walls and bundles to raise order value. Use when planning collections or bundles.
---
- A collection = 6–12 designs sharing palette, technique and mood; sets of 3 share composition rhythm.
- Bundles: set of 3 (≈2.2× single price), gallery wall of 6–9 (≈3.5–4×). Check `brand/pricing.md`.
- Gallery wall: mix 2–3 ratios, one anchor piece, consistent palette; produce a gallery-wall mockup.
- When a SKU sells, propose 2 siblings (same collection) to the curator.
- Keep `brand/collections.md` up to date: name, story, palette, SKUs.
